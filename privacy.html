Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-23
Last updated: 2026-05-23

1. Overview

EndlessWit ("we," "our," "us") operates a small independent t-shirt business and the internal content management tools we use to manage our own social media presence. This Privacy Policy describes what data we handle in the course of operating those tools.

2. We do not collect data from third parties

Our content management tool is a single-operator tool used only by EndlessWit owners and authorized representatives against EndlessWit-owned accounts. We do not provide this tool as a service to any third party. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from any user other than the authorized EndlessWit owner-operator.

3. Data we process

In the course of operating our brand accounts, our tools handle the following categories of data, all related to EndlessWit's own accounts and content:

  • Authentication credentials: developer API keys and OAuth access tokens for EndlessWit's own accounts on third-party platforms (TikTok, Meta/Instagram, etc.). Stored locally and used only to authenticate API requests on EndlessWit's behalf.
  • Content we create and publish: captions, graphics, videos, and accompanying metadata for posts on EndlessWit's accounts.
  • Aggregate engagement metrics: view counts, like counts, and comment counts for our own posts, retrieved via official platform APIs. We do not retrieve or store individual user identities of viewers, likers, or commenters.

4. Data storage

All data is stored locally on EndlessWit-controlled infrastructure. We do not transmit personal data to any third party other than the platform APIs we use to publish content (TikTok, Meta, and similar), and only to the extent required by those APIs to perform the publishing or analytics actions we initiate.

5. Third-party platforms

When we use third-party platforms (TikTok, Instagram, and others) to publish content, those platforms have their own privacy policies governing data they collect about viewers of our content. Please review each platform's policy:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy (covers Instagram)

6. Your rights

If you are a viewer of our content and have concerns about content that includes or references you, please contact us at endlesswits@gmail.com and we will respond promptly and in good faith. We will honor reasonable requests to remove or update content under our control.

7. Cookies and tracking

We do not operate a consumer-facing website that uses cookies or tracking technologies. The static policy pages you are reading right now are hosted on GitHub Pages and may be subject to GitHub's hosting privacy practices — see GitHub's general privacy statement.

8. Children's privacy

EndlessWit's products and content are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention

We retain content and engagement data for as long as it is useful for managing our brand presence and product performance, typically up to several years. Authentication credentials are retained only as long as they are valid for the relevant third-party platform.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent change. Material changes will be summarized in a note near the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at endlesswits@gmail.com.
