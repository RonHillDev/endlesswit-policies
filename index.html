About our content tool

We operate an internal content management tool that helps the EndlessWit owner create, review, and publish branded humor content to EndlessWit's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram). All posts are reviewed and approved by a human owner-operator before publishing. The tool uses official platform developer APIs and is used exclusively against EndlessWit-owned accounts — it is not provided as a service to any third party.
